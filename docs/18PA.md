# 18PA implementation

This branch extends the existing 18PA map and company definitions into a playable
alpha implementation. It includes rule regressions, browser rendering checks,
and a complete game fixture. Human playtesting and upstream review are still
needed before production release.

## Rules sources

- David G. D. Hecht, **18PA rules, version 1.0, 7 July 2015**, available from
  [Golden Spike Games](https://goldenspikegames.com/). The rules URL is also in
  `lib/engine/game/g_18_pa/meta.rb`.
- The rules take precedence over player aids. The depot has five purchasable
  2-trains and four purchasable 3-trains, in addition to private and NYC trains.
- [Designer clarification on 2R transfers](https://groups.google.com/g/dtg-proto/c/R6LY7q2jS8Y):
  2R trains stay with their acquiring company. This clarification predates the
  published rules and is retained as the interpretation for alpha review.
- [Published-game track allowances](https://groups.google.com/g/dtg-proto/c/OlXig1JwdNQ),
  confirmed against the charters by co-developer Ian D. Wilson: public companies
  lay two yellows or one upgrade; privates lay one yellow or one green upgrade.

## Implemented

- Auction for the right to select a private, with premiums above $110; unsold
  privates can be bought in stock rounds.
- Nine operating private railways with built-in trains and half payouts.
- Five-share public companies, full capitalization, conversion to ten shares,
  and optional purchases immediately after conversion.
- NYC's reserved shares and train, preformation share purchases, private
  exchanges, presidency priority, delayed activation, and $770 capitalization.
- Local acquisition offers and owner decisions, replacement stations, increased
  holding limits, and permanent 2R trains from phase 5.
- Destination blockers and doubling, Scranton mining rights, Fall River ferry,
  phase-dependent station bonuses, 3D trains, emergency funding, and player debt.

## Audit checkpoint: 5 September 2026

Recovered source changes were uncommitted and had no tests. The audit fixed:

- An auction state collision that suppressed bidding, and a missing method used
  by the auction's player cash display.
- A route station check that assumed towns had token slots.
- NYC's reserved train being offered before the company became active.
- Conversion share menus using fractional division in compiled JavaScript.
- Duplicate-station cleanup preserving the home station, and destination checks
  running immediately after track construction.
- Invalid depot prices, train trades after emergency share sales, and share-sale
  proceeds exceeding the amount required for the selected depot train.

Focused validation passes:

- **29 rules examples** covering auctions, capitalization, conversion, NYC
  priority and operating order, local-owner consent, destinations, ferry
  construction and closure, station cleanup, emergency funding, debt, and a
  stock-round bank break.
- **14 browser rendering examples** covering the new choices and special steps,
  plus the finished game, using the actual compiled Opal application.
- **19 fixture checks** including strict and ordinary replay, exact final scores,
  total cash conservation and debt reconciliation.
- **502-action complete game**, ending naturally at the bank break in phase 3D.
  It includes all six conversions, all six local acquisitions, NYC formation,
  mining rights, train rusting and the complete train sequence.
- **Full repository suite: 9,359 examples, zero failures**, using four workers
  (the same test suite as `rake spec_parallel`).
- **RuboCop: 2,531 files, zero offenses.** All game assets compile successfully.
- Upstream `master` remains at the branch's base commit, so the submission diff
  contains only the 18PA implementation, documentation and tests.

The fixture is `public/fixtures/18PA/18PA_game_end_bank.json`. On a running copy
of this branch, open `/fixture/18PA/18PA_game_end_bank` to inspect it. The game
also appears in the new-game selector as an alpha title.

## Submission and resumption

### Human playtest, 6 September 2026

A five-player hotseat game reached OR 4.1. The transcript and screenshots cover
NYC formation and later activation, conversions, local acquisition, a permanent
2R alongside two ordinary trains, 3D town exclusion, destination and station
bonuses, mining income, emergency borrowing and stock-round interest. Borrowing
$20 produced $30 debt, then $45 after the stock round; accumulated debt of $868
grew to $1,302. This is human playtest evidence, not a complete action replay.

Playtest fixes: enable the train-buying UI's personal-loan warning instead of
bankruptcy language, and include the once-per-OR Scranton bonus in the submit
preview with a breakdown. The latter changes display only, not stored revenue.
There are now 31 focused rule tests.

Remaining useful human checks: debt repayment and restored stock purchasing;
a 3D through an active doubling destination; the Fall River ferry before closure;
and completing the game through bank break and final debt scoring. The opening
auction was passed in this playtest, so competitive bidding remains another
useful check. Automated coverage does not replace multiplayer playtesting.
